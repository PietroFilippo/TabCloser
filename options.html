Temporary reveals

Open “Why hidden?” on a replacement, then hold to reveal. Each post gets at most three seconds total per day, shared across its images and all tabs. Letting go or leaving the tab hides it again; videos stay paused.

or until

Resets at local midnight. Lock this allowance independently, including zero to keep reveals off. While this lock or an X protection lock is active, the allowance may only decrease. Refreshes preserve usage; an interrupted reveal may use its full reservation.

Manual hides

Right-click an X post or its media, then choose TabCloser → hide this post’s text or hide this image / video. Author details and post actions remain visible. Older whole-post choices now hide text and media individually. Choices stay on this device even when automatic protection is off. Removing them is unavailable while X protection is locked.
